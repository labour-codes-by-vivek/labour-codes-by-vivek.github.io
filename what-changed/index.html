Recent changes

The law keeps moving. These are the changes since the Codes began on 21 November 2025, newest first.

Official sources last checked on 1 Nov 2026. To be told of changes, add this feed to a feed reader.

The main changes

  1. 28 Oct 2026

    National Social Security Board constituted

    The Central Government has constituted the Board for unorganised workers, for three years from publication.

    S.O. 5884(E) · Social Security Code s. 6

  2. 15 Oct 2026

    Pension Scheme: the Central Government's share

    The Central Government's contribution to the Pension Scheme is limited to wages up to ₹15,000 a month, from 17 September 2026. The employer's share still follows the wage ceiling.

    G.S.R. 890(E) · Employees' Pension Scheme, 2026

  3. 28 Sept 2026

    ESI reaches more districts

    ESI contributions and benefits begin on 1 October 2026 in more districts of Gujarat and Madhya Pradesh.

    S.O. 5310(E): Gujarat · S.O. 5313(E): Madhya Pradesh

  4. 17 Sept 2026

    EPF wage ceiling raised to ₹25,000 a month

    The wage ceiling for the provident fund Chapter is now ₹25,000 a month. It replaces the ₹15,000 notified in May 2026.

    S.O. 5109(E) · S.O. 2702(E), replaced

  5. 25 Aug 2026

    Bonus wage limits notified

    Employees earning up to ₹21,000 a month are eligible for bonus, and bonus is worked out on ₹7,000 or the minimum wage. Both are deemed in force from 21 November 2025.

    S.O. 4711(E) · S.O. 4710(E) · Bonus guide

  6. 25 Aug 2026

    Tribunal rules changed in the Industrial Relations Code

    Section 44(4) and (9) were replaced by the Tribunals Reforms Act, 2026. See the before and after below.

    Industrial Relations Code s. 44

  7. 1 Jul 2026

    Provident fund, pension and insurance rates notified

    Twelve per cent for provident fund, deemed from 21 November 2025, with some establishments left out. Eight and one-third per cent of wages goes to the Pension Fund, and the employer pays one-half per cent to the Insurance Fund.

    S.O. 3582(E) · S.O. 3580(E) · S.O. 3581(E)

  8. 29 Jun 2026

    New EPF, Pension and Insurance Schemes

    Three new Schemes under the Code on Social Security replace the Schemes of 1952, 1971, 1995 and 1976.

    Employees' Provident Funds Scheme · Employees' Pension Scheme · Employees' Deposit-Linked Insurance Scheme

  9. 8 May 2026

    Central Rules and Model Standing Orders made final

    The Central Government notified the final Rules under all four Codes, and Model Standing Orders.

    Central Rules · Model Standing Orders

  10. 16 Feb 2026

    Industrial Relations Code amended on the repeal of the old Acts

    Section 104(1) was replaced, and tribunals and authorities under the old Acts continue until those under the Code begin to work. In force from 21 November 2025.

    Industrial Relations Code s. 104 · S.O. 465(E)

  11. 30 Jan 2026

    Code on Wages: the limit for supervisors

    A person in a supervisory capacity earning above ₹18,000 a month is not a worker under the Code on Wages.

    S.O. 454(E) · Code on Wages s. 2

  12. 21 Nov 2025

    The four Labour Codes begin

    The Codes came into force on 21 November 2025. Parts of the Code on Wages and the Code on Social Security were left for later.

    S.O. 5322(E): Code on Wages · S.O. 5320(E): Industrial Relations · S.O. 5319(E): Social Security · S.O. 5321(E): Safety

Sections reworded: before and after

Industrial Relations Code s. 44: Industrial Tribunal

Changed by The Tribunals Reforms Act, 2026 (No. 17 of 2026), section 18 and the Second Schedule, item 13, in force from 25 Aug 2026: for sub-section (4), the following sub-section shall be substituted.

Before

(4) The qualifications for appointment, method of recruitment, term of office, salaries and allowances, resignation, removal and the other terms of conditions of service of the Judicial Member and the Administrative Member of the Tribunal constituted by the Central Government shall be in accordance with the rules made under section 184 of the Finance Act, 2017:

Provided that a person who has held a post below the rank of Joint Secretary to the Government of India or an equivalent rank in the Central Government or a State Government, shall not be eligible to be appointed as an Administrative Member of the Tribunal.

Now

(4) Notwithstanding anything contained in this Act, the qualifications, manner of selection, appointment, salaries and allowances, resignation, removal and other conditions of service and eligibility for reappointment of Judicial Member and Administrative Member of an Industrial Tribunal under this Act shall be governed by the provisions of the Tribunals Reforms Act, 2026.

Industrial Relations Code s. 44: Industrial Tribunal

Changed by The Tribunals Reforms Act, 2026 (No. 17 of 2026), section 18 and the Second Schedule, item 13, in force from 25 Aug 2026: for sub-section (9), the following sub-section shall be substituted.

Before

(9) If, for any reason, a vacancy (other than a temporary absence) occurs in a National Industrial Tribunal or a Tribunal, then, such vacancy shall be filled up in such manner as may be prescribed, without prejudice to the provisions of sub-section (4) or sub-section (5), as the case may be, and the proceeding shall be continued before such National Industrial Tribunal or Tribunal, as the case may be, from the stage at which the vacancy is filled.

Now

(9) If, for any reason, a vacancy (other than a temporary absence) occurs in a National Industrial Tribunal or the Tribunal constituted by the State Government under sub-section (1), then, such vacancy shall be filled up in such manner as may be prescribed, without prejudice to sub-section (5), and the proceeding shall be continued before such National Industrial Tribunal or the Tribunal constituted by the State Government, from the stage at which the vacancy is filled.

Industrial Relations Code s. 104: Repeal and savings

Changed by The Industrial Relations Code (Amendment) Act, 2026 (No. 1 of 2026), section 2, in force from 21 Nov 2025: for sub-section (1), the following sub-sections shall be substituted.

Before

(1) In the notification issued under sub-section (3) of section 1 for the commencement of any provision of this Code, the Central Government may specify that the provisions of—

(a) the Trade Unions Act, 1926;

(b) the Industrial Employment (Standing Orders) Act, 1946; and

(c) the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947,

shall stand repealed with effect from the date appointed in the notification in this behalf and the remaining provisions of the enactments referred to in clauses (a) to (c) shall remain in force till they are repealed by like notifications in the like manner.

Now

(1) The following enactments shall stand repealed on and from the date appointed in the notification issued under sub-section (3) of section 1, namely:—

(a) the Trade Unions Act, 1926;

(b) the Industrial Employment (Standing Orders) Act, 1946; and

(c) the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947.

(1A) Notwithstanding such repeal under sub-section (1), the functioning of the Tribunals and statutory authorities functioning under the Acts so repealed shall continue to function till such Tribunals and other statutory authorities becomes functional under this Code.

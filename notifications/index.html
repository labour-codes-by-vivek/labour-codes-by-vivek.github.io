Government notifications

Notifications under the Labour Codes

Government orders that start the Codes and set figures like wage ceilings.

  • 156 notifications

The Codes and the Central Rules leave many things to be fixed "by notification": figures and rates, the officers who enforce them, forms and procedures, and the dates provisions come into force. These are the Central notifications issued under the Codes and published by the Labour Ministry since the Codes came into force on 21 November 2025, along with the earlier commencement notifications. Each one has its own page with its English text word for word, the provision it was made under, and the Gazette PDF.

Drafts published for comment are listed separately and marked "not law". Notifications issued by State Governments are not included.

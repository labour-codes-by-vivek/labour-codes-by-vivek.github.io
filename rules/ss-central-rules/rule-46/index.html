The Social Security (Central) Rules, 2026

Rule 46. Benefits of beneficiary

Chapter VII — Social Security and Cess in Respect of Building and Other Construction WorkersMade under The Code on Social Security, 2020G.S.R. 344(E), 8 May 2026

Official text

If any building worker has been a beneficiary for at least three years continuously immediately before attaining the age of sixty years, for the benefit applicable to such worker, the Central Government may formulate such schemes as may be notified by the Central Government under which such worker shall be eligible to get such benefit.

Source: The Social Security (Central) Rules, 2026, G.S.R. 344(E) — Gazette of India, p. 187 of the PDF. Open our copy at p. 187 · official download · all sources

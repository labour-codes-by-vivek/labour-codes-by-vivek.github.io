Act

The Occupational Safety, Health and Working Conditions Code, 2020

An Act to consolidate and amend the laws regulating the occupational safety, health and working conditions of the persons employed in an establishment and for matters connected therewith or incidental thereto.

  • Act No. 37 of 2020
  • Assented 28th September, 2020
  • Gazette CG-DL-E-29092020-222112
  • 14 chapters
  • 143 sections
  • 3 schedules

Commencement In force

All 143 sections are in force since 21 Nov 2025. Each section page shows the notification wording that applies to it.

For employers

What wage records must an employer keep?

  • Keep a register of the persons employed, the muster roll and wages s. 50(1).
  • Under the Central Rules, these can be kept electronically or on paper, in the Forms the Rules set out rule 51(1).
  • Failing to maintain records, or maintaining them improperly, can bring a fine of up to ₹10,000 s. 54(2).

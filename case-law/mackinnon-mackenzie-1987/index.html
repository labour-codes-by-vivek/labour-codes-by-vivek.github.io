Supreme Court of India

Mackinnon Mackenzie & Co. Ltd. v. Audrey D'Costa

Decided 26 Mar 1987[1987] 2 S.C.R. 6591987 INSC 81Special Leave Petition (Civil) No. 1265 of 1987Bench: E.S. Venkataramiah, M.M. Dutt

Whether women stenographers paid less than men for the same work were denied equal remuneration.

Decided under an earlier law

The Court decided this case under the Equal Remuneration Act, 1976. Section 69 of the Code on Wages, 2019 repeals the Equal Remuneration Act, 1976. The Court's words are shown below beside the Code provision this library compares them with. This library does not say whether the decision holds good under the Code: compare the two texts.

What the Court decided

Each point is this library's summary, followed by the Court's own words, checked word for word against the Supreme Court's typed copy of the judgment (JUDIS file 8771). The pinpoint gives the page of the Supreme Court Reports and the margin letter (A to H) printed beside the line where the words begin.

  1. Whether jobs are the same or of a similar nature is judged broadly, on the duties actually performed; differences of detail should not defeat a claim to equal pay on trivial grounds.

  2. A settlement between the union and the management could not justify the lower pay: the Act has effect notwithstanding any award, agreement or contract of service.

  3. An employer's inability to pay is no defence: whether the Act applies does not depend on the employer's financial ability to pay equal remuneration.

The earlier provision and the Code

Equal Remuneration Act, 1976, section 4(1)

As quoted in the judgment: [1987] 2 S.C.R. 659, at 669 C · open the report at p. 669

No employer shall pay to any worker, employed by him in an establishment or employment, remuneration, whether payable in cash or in kind, at rates less favourable than those at which remuneration is paid by him to the workers of the opposite sex in such establishment or employment for performing the same work or work of a similar nature.

Code on Wages, section 3(1)

Prohibition of discrimination on ground of gender In force

(1) There shall be no discrimination in an establishment or any unit thereof among employees on the ground of gender in matters relating to wages by the same employer, in respect of the same work or work of a similar nature done by any employee.

Wording in common: 35% of the words, in the same order.

Wording differs; the decision may not carry over. The Code provision is not worded like the provision the Court interpreted.

Supreme Court of India

Bijay Cotton Mills Ltd. v. State of Ajmer

Decided 14 Oct 1954[1955] 1 S.C.R. 7521954 INSC 92Petitions Nos. 188 and 189 of 1954 under Article 32Bench: Mehr Chand Mahajan, B.K. Mukherjea, Vivian Bose, B. Jagannadhadas, T.L. Venkatarama Aiyyar

Whether the Minimum Wages Act, which makes it an offence to pay less than the minimum wages fixed under it, puts unreasonable restrictions on employers and workers.

Decided under an earlier law

The Court decided this case on the Constitution: whether the restrictions the Minimum Wages Act, 1948 puts on the freedom of trade or business are reasonable under Article 19. Section 69 of the Code on Wages, 2019 repeals the Minimum Wages Act, 1948. The Court's words are shown below, with the Code provision this library lists the case on. This library does not say whether the decision holds good under the Code.

What the Court decided

Each point is this library's summary, followed by the Court's own words, checked word for word against the Supreme Court's typed copy of the judgment (JUDIS file 888). The pinpoint gives the page of the Supreme Court Reports and the margin letter (A to H) printed beside the line where the words begin.

  1. Restraints on freedom of contract that secure minimum wages and protect workers against exploitation are not unreasonable, and employers cannot complain of having to pay minimum wages even where workers, out of poverty, would work for less.

  2. An employer's good intentions, or difficulty in paying, are irrelevant: they are no reason to strike the law down.

  3. Consulting committees and advisory bodies, on which employers and employees are equally represented, is an adequate safeguard against hasty or capricious fixing of wages by the Government.

  4. The restrictions, though they interfere to some extent with the freedom of trade or business, are reasonable and in the interest of the general public.

The Code

This library lists the case on: Code on Wages, section 5 (Payment of minimum rate of wages In force) · Code on Wages, section 8 (Procedure for fixing and revising minimum wages In force).

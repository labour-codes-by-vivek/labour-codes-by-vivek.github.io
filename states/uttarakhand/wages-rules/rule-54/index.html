The Uttarakhand Code on Wages Rules, 2026

Rule 54. Uploading on the official website of the department

Chapter 7 — MiscellaneousMade under The Code on Wages, 2019No. 1115/VIII-1/2026-60(Shram)/2019, 25 Aug 2026

Text read by machine from the official scan

These rules will be uploaded on the official website of the Labour Department within one month from the date of their final notification.

Source: The Uttarakhand Code on Wages Rules, 2026, No. 1115/VIII-1/2026-60(Shram)/2019 — Uttarakhand Gazette Extraordinary, 25 August 2026, p. 85 of the PDF. Open our copy at p. 85 · official download · all sources

The official copy is a scan. This text was read by machine and only tidied by program, not checked page by page, so it will contain mistakes, including in numbers: the official PDF is the copy to rely on. The Forms and the Schedules of employments are not reproduced here; read them in the PDF. This is the English translation the Governor ordered published under Article 348(3) of the Constitution; the Hindi text in the same file could not be machine-read. Printed page 79, which holds rules 37 to 39 and possibly the end of rule 36, is missing from the official file, so those rules are not here. The places where rules 14, 15 and 19 to 55 begin, and their margin headings, were read against the scan and set right by hand on 21 November 2026. The headings are printed in the margin of the scan and may be incomplete here.

The Mizoram Occupational Safety, Health and Working Conditions Rules, 2026

Rule 134. Special safeguards for the employment of women and adolescents in using or handling hazardous chemicals under Section 93

Chapter XIII — Plantation WorkersMade under The Occupational Safety, Health and Working Conditions Code, 2020No. B-11011/12/2026-LESDE, 12 Aug 2026

Official text

Prohibition and restriction on the employment of women and adolescent workers in hazardous occupations involving the use or handling of hazardous chemicals— No woman worker or adolescent worker shall be engaged in the handling, storing, or transportation of insecticides, chemicals, and toxic substances by the employer in his plantations.

Source: The Mizoram Occupational Safety, Health and Working Conditions Rules, 2026, No. B-11011/12/2026-LESDE — Mizoram Gazette Extraordinary, Vol. LV, Issue No. 674, 13 August 2026, p. 58 of the PDF. Open our copy at p. 58 · official download · all sources

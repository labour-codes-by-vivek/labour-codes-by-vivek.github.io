The Uttar Pradesh Code on Social Security Rules, 2026

Rule 28. Salary and allowances to official and non-official members

Chapter II — Social Security OrganizationPart II — The Uttar Pradesh Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare BoardMade under The Code on Social Security, 2020Notification No. 1431/XXXVI-3-2026-1399661, 27 Aug 2026

Official text

(1) The salary, travelling allowance and daily allowance of an official member shall be governed by the rules applicable to him against his post, journey performed by him on official duties and shall be paid by the Authority paying his salary.

(2) Non-Official Members of the Board shall be paid travelling allowance and daily allowance for attending meetings of the Board at such rates as are admissible to lowest of Group “A” Officer of the State Government.

Source: The Uttar Pradesh Code on Social Security Rules, 2026, Notification No. 1431/XXXVI-3-2026-1399661 — Uttar Pradesh Gazette Extraordinary, 27 August 2026, p. 102 of the PDF. Open our copy at p. 102 · official download · all sources

The rules were made in Hindi. The English text is the translation published by order of the Governor under clause (3) of Article 348 of the Constitution, which makes it the authoritative text in English. Rule headings are printed as marginal notes.

  • S.O. 23
  • Notified 30 Jun 2026
  • Rajasthan Gazette Extraordinary, Part 4 (Ga), Sub-section (II)
  • 16 chapters
  • 130 rules

Made under The Occupational Safety, Health and Working Conditions Code, 2020.

Notification text (preamble)

NOTIFICATION Jaipur, june 30, 2026 S.O.23 .- Whereas, the draft of the Rajasthan Occupational Safety, Health and Working Conditions Rules, 2026 was published as required by sub-section (1) of section 133, sub-section (1) of section 135 and section 137 of the Occupational Safety, Health and Working Conditions Code, 2020 (Central Act No. 37 of 2020) in the Rajasthan Gazette Extraordinary Part 4 (ga) up-khand (II) dated 03.02.2026 inviting objections and suggestions from all persons likely to be effected thereby, before expiry of forty five days from the date on which copies of the Official Gazette containing the said draft rules were made available to the public; And whereas, copies of the Official Gazette in which the said draft rules were published and made available to the public on 03.02. 2026;

And whereas, objections and suggestions received from the public on the said draft rules were considered by the State Government;

Now, therefore in exercise of the powers conferred by section 133 and 135 of the Occupational Safety, Health and Working Conditions Code, 2020 (Central Act No. 37 of 2020), the State Government hereby makes the following rules, namely:-

The Arunachal Pradesh Occupational Safety, Health and Working Conditions Rules, 2022

  • No. LAB(W) 03/2020
  • Notified 1 Apr 2022
  • Arunachal Pradesh Gazette Extraordinary No. 132, Vol. XXIX, 2 May 2022
  • 9 chapters
  • 91 rules
  • 90 forms and annexes

Made under The Occupational Safety, Health and Working Conditions Code, 2020.

Notification text (preamble)

DEPARTMENT OF LABOUR AND EMPLOYMENT ITANAGAR ——————— NOTIFICATION The 1st April, 2022

No. LAB (W) 03/2020.—— Whereas the draft Rules, as required under Sections 133 and 135 of the Code on Occupational Safety, Health and Working Conditions, read with section 24 of General Clauses Act, 1897(10 of 1897) published in the Arunachal Pradesh official Gazette No. 287, Vol. XXVII, Naharlagun, dated 23rd December, 2021 inviting objections and suggestions from the persons likely to be affected, before expiry of the period of forty five days. And whereas objections and suggestions from the persons likely be affected before expiry of the said period of forty five days have been considered by the State Government. Now, the State Government in exercise of the powers conferred on it by sub-Section (1) of Sections 133 and 135 of the Code on Occupational Safety, Health and Working Conditions, 2020 (No. 37of 2020) read with sub-Section (2) of the Sections 133 and 135, and in supersession of -1. The Arunachal Pradesh Building and Other Construction Workers (Regulation of Employment and Condition of Services)Rules, 2006 and amendment 2016; 2. The Contract Labour (Regulation and Abolition) Arunachal Pradesh Rules,1995; and 3. The Inter-State Migrant Workmen (Regulation of Employment and Conditions of Service) Arunachal Pradesh Rules, 1993; and 4. The Arunachal Pradesh Factories Rules, 2012 except as respects things done or omitted to be done before such supersession, hereby makes the following Rules, namely:-

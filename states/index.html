State and UT rules under the Labour Codes

State rules apply where the State or Union Territory Government is the appropriate Government under the Code. Choose yours to see its rules, final or draft.

Where a State has no final rules yet, the Labour Ministry's reply is: “As per the provisions of Section 6 of General Clauses Act, 1897, old rules will remain in force till final notification of new rules under the Code, to the extent these are in line with Codes.” FAQ 1, FAQs on Labour Codes, 30 Dec 2025: official copy · our copy. This is the Ministry's explanation, not the law.

How this was checked

Only State or UT government sites (labour departments, gazettes, government-order registers) are cited. Third-party trackers were used as leads only; where they disagree with the official sites, the note says so. Where trackers report final rules that the official sites do not show, the State's page says so.

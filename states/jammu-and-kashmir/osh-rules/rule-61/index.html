Confidentiality of information. - i. The occupier of a factory carrying on 'hazardous process' shall disclose all information needed for protecting safety and health of the workers and the general public in the neighbourhood—

An occupier aggrieved by an order of Chief Inspector-cumFacilitator may prefer an appeal before the Government of Jammu and Kashmir within a period of 30 days. The Jammu and Kashmir Government shall give an opportunity to the occupier of being heard and pass an order. The order of the Government shall be final.

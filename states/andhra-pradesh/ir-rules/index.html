The Industrial Relations (Andhra Pradesh) Rules, 2026

  • G.O.Rt. No. 111
  • Notified 12 Jun 2026
  • Andhra Pradesh Gazette Extraordinary (as ordered by G.O.Rt. No. 111)
  • 13 chapters
  • 53 rules
  • 46 forms and annexes

Text from the Government Order that orders the notification's publication in the Andhra Pradesh Gazette; the Gazette issue itself was not found on the AP e-Gazette.

Made under The Industrial Relations Code, 2020.

Notification text (preamble)

Whereas, the draft of the Industrial Relations (Andhra Pradesh) Rules, 2026, as required under sub-section (1) of section 99 of the Industrial Relations Code, 2020 (Act 35 of 2020), were published in the Andhra Pradesh Gazette No.(627), dated: 16.03.2026 inviting objections and suggestions from all persons likely to be affected thereby within a period of thirty days from the date on which copies of the Gazette containing the said notification were made available to be public; And whereas, the said draft notification were made available to the public on the 16th March, 2026; And whereas, objections and suggestions received from persons and stakeholders have been duly considered by the State Government. Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 99 of the Industrial Relations Code, 2020 (Act 35 of 2020) and in supersession of the previous Rules made by the State Government viz., (1) The Andhra Pradesh Industrial Disputes Rules, 1958, (2) The Andhra Pradesh Trade Unions Regulations, 1927 and (3) The Andhra Pradesh Industrial Employment (Standing Orders) Rules, 1953 except as respects of things done or omitted to be done before such supersession, the State Government hereby makes the following rules, namely,-

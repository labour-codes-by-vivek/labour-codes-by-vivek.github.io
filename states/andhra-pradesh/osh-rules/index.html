The Occupational Safety, Health and Working Conditions (Andhra Pradesh) Rules, 2026

  • G.O.Rt. No. 146
  • Notified 6 Aug 2026
  • Andhra Pradesh Gazette Extraordinary (as ordered by G.O.Rt. No. 146)
  • 19 chapters
  • 174 rules
  • 85 forms and annexes

Text from the Government Order that orders the notification's publication in the Andhra Pradesh Gazette; the Gazette issue itself was not found on the AP e-Gazette.

The Rules go from rule 147 to rule 149; there is no rule 148.

Made under The Occupational Safety, Health and Working Conditions Code, 2020.

Notification text (preamble)

Whereas, the draft of the Occupational Safety, Health and Working Conditions Code, (Andhra Pradesh) Rules, 2026 as required under section 137 of the Occupational Safety, Health and Working Conditions Code, 2020 (Central Act No.37 of 2020), were published in the Andhra Pradesh Gazette No.(3), dated: 06.04.2026 inviting objections and suggestions from all persons likely to be affected thereby within a period of Forty Five (45) days from the date on which copies of the Gazette containing the said notification were made available to be public;

And whereas, the said draft preliminary notification were made available to the public on the 6th April, 2026;

And whereas, objections and suggestions received from persons and stakeholders have been duly considered by the State Government.

Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 133 and 135 of the Occupational Safety, Health and Working Conditions Code, 2020 (Central Act No.37 of 2020), and in supersession of the previous Rules made by the State Government viz., (1).The Beedi and Cigar Workers (Conditions of Employment) Act, 1966 (Central Act No.32 of 1966),(2). The Building and Other Construction Workers (Regulation of Employment and Conditions of Service) Act, 1996 (Central Act No.27 of 1996), (3). Contract Labour(Regulation and Abolition) Act, 1970 (Central Act No.37 of 1970), (4). Dock Workers (Safety, Health and Welfare) Act, 1986 (Central Act No.54 of 1986), (5). the Factories Act, 1948 (Central Act No. 63 of 1948), (6). the Inter-State Migrant Workmen (Regulation of Employment and Conditions of Service) Act, 1979 (Central Act No.30 of 1979), (7). The Motor Transport Workers Act, 1961 (Central Act No.27 of 1961), and (8). The Plantation Labour Act, 1951 (Central Act No. 69 of 1951) except as respects of things done or omitted to be done before such supersession, the State Government hereby makes the following rules, namely,-

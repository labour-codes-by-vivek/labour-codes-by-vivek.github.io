The Code on Social Security (Andhra Pradesh) Rules, 2026

Rule 10. Other welfare measures and facilities

Chapter IIMade under The Code on Social Security, 2020G.O.Rt. No. 126, 7 Jul 2026

Official text

If, in the opinion of the Central Government, it is necessary, for the improvement of well-being of the building workers, to formulate a new welfare scheme or schemes in a State, the Central Government may formulate such scheme as determined by that Government from time to time.

C. GENERAL

Source: The Code on Social Security (Andhra Pradesh) Rules, 2026, G.O.Rt. No. 126 — Andhra Pradesh Gazette Extraordinary (as ordered by G.O.Rt. No. 126), p. 7 of the PDF. Open our copy at p. 7 · official download · all sources

Text from the Government Order that orders the notification's publication in the Andhra Pradesh Gazette; the Gazette issue itself was not found on the AP e-Gazette.

The Code on Social Security (Lakshadweep) Rules, 2025

Rule 1. Short title, extent and commencement

Chapter I — PreliminaryMade under The Code on Social Security, 2020F. No. 08/03/2024-LE&T/72, 25 Mar 2026

Official text

(1) These rules may be called the Code on Social Security (Lakshadweep) Rules, 2025.

(2) They extend to the whole of the Union Territory of Lakshadweep.

(3) They shall come into force after the date of their final publication in the Official Gazette of Lakshadweep.

Source: The Code on Social Security (Lakshadweep) Rules, 2025, F. No. 08/03/2024-LE&T/72 — Lakshadweep Gazette Extraordinary Vol. LXI, No. 61, p. 1 of the PDF. Open our copy at p. 1 · official download · all sources

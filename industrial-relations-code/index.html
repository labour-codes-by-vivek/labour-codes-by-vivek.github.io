Act

The Industrial Relations Code, 2020

An Act to consolidate and amend the laws relating to Trade Unions, conditions of employment in industrial establishment or undertaking, investigation and settlement of industrial disputes and for matters connected therewith or incidental thereto.

  • Act No. 35 of 2020
  • Assented 28th September, 2020
  • Gazette CG-DL-E-29092020-222118
  • 14 chapters
  • 104 sections
  • 3 schedules

Commencement In force

All 104 sections are in force since 21 Nov 2025. Each section page shows the notification wording that applies to it.

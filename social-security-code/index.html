Act

The Code on Social Security, 2020

An Act to amend and consolidate the laws relating to social security with the goal to extend social security to all employees and workers either in the organised or unorganised or any other sectors and for matters connected therewith or incidental thereto.

  • Act No. 36 of 2020
  • Assented 28th September, 2020
  • Gazette CG-DL-E-29092020-222111
  • 14 chapters
  • 164 sections
  • 7 schedules

Commencement In force

All 164 sections are in force: some from 3 May 2021, all from 21 Nov 2025. Each section page shows the notification wording that applies to it.

Facilities and staff · OSH Code

Manager of a mine

  • All the time
  • Employer

What to do

Appoint a sole manager with the qualifications set out in the Rules; no mine may be opened, re-opened or worked without a duly appointed manager rule 107 s. 67(1).

Who it applies to

Every mine s. 67(1).

When

Before the mine is opened, re-opened or worked rule 107.

How

  • The certificate of competency required depends on the type of mine and its average output or material handled rule 107.

  • The owner or agent may act as manager if they hold the qualifications s. 67(1), proviso.

  • Confirm in writing forthwith any instructions the owner or agent gives the manager s. 67(2).

Provisions cited (2)

  • OSH Code, s. 67— Managers In force
  • OSH (Central) Rules, r. 107— Qualification and appointment of manager in mines

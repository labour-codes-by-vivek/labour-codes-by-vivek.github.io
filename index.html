India's Labour Codes, explained simply, with the official text

Find out what the law says about your job or your workplace. Every answer cites the section it comes from, so you can read the law itself.

Short answers written in advance and checked against the law, with the exact sections. Questions are saved without your name, to improve the answers.

Search the Codes, Rules, State Rules, notifications and guides. Try a topic in your own words (“maternity leave”), a section of a Code (“social security section 53”), a rule (“wages rule 51”), a State (“Gujarat”), an old Act section (“Industrial Disputes Act 25F”), or a notification number (“S.O. 5109”). Short forms work too, such as “SS 53” for Social Security Code section 53.

How you can check this library. The law is copied word for word from official PDFs, and each page links to its source and says whether it is in force. Seven State Rules sets are the exception: their official copies are scans, so the text is read by machine and says so on every page. Guides cite every point, but no lawyer has reviewed them.
